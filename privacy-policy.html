Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 14, 2025

VerseStream (“we,” “us,” “our,” or “Company”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our application (the “App”).

1. Scope & Acceptance

By downloading, installing, or using the VerseStream App on any Android device, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. We may update this policy at any time, and changes will take effect upon posting within the App. Your continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

2. Information We Collect

a. Authentication & Personal Data

  • Email Address: We collect your email address via Google OAuth for authentication purposes only.
  • Account ID: An internal user ID linked to your email is stored to manage user settings and sessions.
  • Username and Profile Picture:: We collect your username (e.g., Alex Smith) and profile picture via Google OAuth for authentication and profile display purposes.

b. Usage Analytics & Technical Data

We automatically collect certain data when you use the App, including:

  • Device information (type, OS version, screen resolution, system language)
  • IP address, approximate city-level location, and network provider
  • In-App actions (e.g., searches, link clicks, timestamps, feature usage)
  • Anonymized crash logs and error diagnostics to improve App performance

c. Preferences & Settings

We store in-App configurations, such as themes, playback quality preferences, parental controls, favorites, and notification settings.

d. Exclusions

We do not collect or store sensitive personal information, including full names (beyond username), phone contacts, calendar data, photos (beyond profile picture), location history, or payment details.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data for the following purposes:

  1. Authentication: To verify your identity via Google OAuth.
  2. Service Delivery: To provide App functionality tailored to your preferences.
  3. Premium Services: To verify eligibility for premium features, such as ad-free experiences or enhanced functionality.
  4. Analytics & Improvement: To analyze usage trends, fix bugs, optimize performance, and develop new features.
  5. Support & Communication: To contact you regarding App updates, maintenance, or support inquiries.
  6. Legal Compliance: To respond to lawful requests, such as court orders or DMCA notices.

We do not use your data for marketing purposes or share it with advertisers.

4. How & When We Share Your Data

a. Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party providers to deliver core App services, including:

  • Authentication services for user login verification
  • Analytics platforms for aggregated usage insights
  • Cloud infrastructure for secure data storage
  • Payment processors for handling subscription payments

All providers are contractually obligated to process data securely and only for the purposes we specify. We do not collect or store payment information; all payment transactions are managed securely by third-party providers.

b. Legal & Compliance Requests

We may disclose your data to comply with valid legal processes (e.g., subpoenas, court orders, DMCA notices) or to protect our legal rights, safety, or property.

c. Business Transactions

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale, your data may be transferred to the new entity. We will notify you of such changes via the App.

d. User-Controlled Sharing

Your data will not be shared beyond the above cases without your explicit consent.

5. Third-Party Content Disclaimer

VerseStream operates solely as an indexing tool that scrapes content in real-time from publicly available third-party websites indexed on Google. We do not host, stream, store, manage, or delete any media content. All links provided in the App direct to external third-party servers. We do not control, endorse, or guarantee the legality, availability, or accuracy of such content. We cannot modify or remove content, as it is dynamically sourced from third-party sites. Your use of third-party content is subject to the respective provider’s terms and applicable copyright laws, not ours.

6. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Restricted access controls and limited administrative privileges
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Anonymized logging for crash and error diagnostics

While we strive to protect your data, no system is entirely secure. We disclaim liability for security breaches beyond our reasonable control.
